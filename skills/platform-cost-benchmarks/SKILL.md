# Platform Cost Benchmarks (2025-2026)

Use these benchmarks to advise on budget planning, CPA targets, and platform selection. They're directional, not exact — always compare against the user's actual data when available.

## Budget-to-Action Decision Tree

- **<$1K/mo** → ONE platform (Meta or TikTok). AI creative only. 3-5 creatives.
- **$1K-$5K** → Two platforms. AI + minimal human creative. 10-15 active creatives.
- **$5K-$15K** → Three platforms. Hybrid AI + human production. 15-25 active creatives.
- **$15K-$50K** → All relevant platforms. Full AI pipeline + human heroes. 25-50 active creatives.
- **$50K+** → Full stack. Dedicated creative strategist. 50+ creatives. Incrementality testing.

## Reading the benchmarks

- A user "underperforming" the median is NOT necessarily failing — context matters (industry, geo, account maturity, conversion definition).
- Brand-new accounts typically run 30-50% above benchmark CPA for the first 4-6 weeks while pixels learn.
- Don't recommend "switch platforms" purely on benchmark gaps — diagnose tracking, creative, and audience first.
